Bring warm candlelight into your Homey routines. Adjust the brightness of your original Bluetooth Yeelight Candela, switch it on or off, and choose a steady glow, gentle breathing or candle flicker to suit the moment. Use Flows to set the mood automatically.

Requires the original Bluetooth Candela (YLFW01YL, micro USB), with its switch in the Bluetooth position and within range of Homey. Candle flicker and breathing are generated by Homey and require the app to keep running. Homey shows the last state commanded by the app; physical twist controls and changes made by other Bluetooth controllers are not synchronized. Models without Bluetooth are not supported.
